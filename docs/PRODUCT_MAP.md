# Product Map

MainStreet owns the customer experience, business workflow, evidence review, approvals, communications, tasks, documents, scheduling, and recovery experience. OpenClaw remains a replaceable untrusted runtime. ClaimSieve owns policy verification, campaign state, permits, containment, execution assurance, ledgers, and portable verification.

The recommended first workflow is a tax-notice case workspace that stops at a submission-ready packet during the pilot. See [`MAINSTREET_PRODUCT_REPORT.md`](MAINSTREET_PRODUCT_REPORT.md).
